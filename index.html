How I work

The same three steps show up in every project above.

  1. 1

    Measure the baseline

    Load test first and record p95 latency, throughput and error rates so every change has something to beat.

  2. 2

    Fix the slow path

    Add the right index, cache or queue where the profile points, and keep writes safe with constraints and idempotency.

  3. 3

    Prove it under load

    Rerun the same benchmark with concurrent clients and keep the change only if the numbers and consistency hold.
